First scan (recording of first-scan.tape)

The user types "kipple" in a terminal with no config file yet. kipple starts the scan
and, at the same moment, opens a four-step welcome.

Step 1 of 4, "How careful should kipple be?". Three levels are listed: Careful, Balanced
(the default) and Thorough. Each one says what it treats as Safe and for how many days a
change counts as recent, and shows how much it would offer on this machine as safe to
clean and to review. The numbers grow while the scan runs; the header says "scan
finished" once it is done. The user moves down to Thorough, back up to Balanced, and
presses enter to choose it.

Step 2 of 4, "Where are your projects?". The project folders kipple found are listed with
checkboxes, followed by "+ add another". The user keeps them as they are and presses
enter.

Step 3 of 4, "Delete or move to Trash by default?". Delete frees the space at once.
Trash can be put back, but frees nothing until the Trash is emptied. This version only
stores the answer: nothing is removed yet. The user moves to Trash and presses enter.

Step 4 of 4, "Prevention tips". Settings of the user's tools that keep less around, which
kipple shows and never changes. The user presses enter to finish.

Finishing saves the answers in one go, so the welcome never shows again, and opens the
home screen. Its header shows the level and how long the scan took. The findings are
sorted into Safe to clean, Review and Protected, each grouped into Agents, Dev and
System, with the detail pane for the finding under the cursor. The user presses q to
quit. Nothing on disk was changed apart from the new config file.

Every step can be skipped with tab, esc goes back a step, and q quits without saving
anything.
